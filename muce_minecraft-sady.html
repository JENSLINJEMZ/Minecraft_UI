📖 CHAPTER I: ORIGIN STORY

EARLY LIFE - SEED: BRENHAM

Sadie Elizabeth Sink was born April 16, 2002 in Brenham, Texas to Lori, a math teacher, and Casey Sink, a football coach. One of 5 kids - 3 older brothers Caleb, Spencer, Mitchell and younger sister Jacey.

Obsessed with High School Musical at 7, she re-enacted it in her garage. Her mom sent her to Houston community theater. Brother Mitchell was taking acting classes - Sadie tagged along and stole the show.

At 10 years old, after auditioning on a whim in NYC, she was cast as Annie on Broadway (2012-2014), alternating the lead role. Then played Young Queen Elizabeth II in The Audience (2015) opposite Helen Mirren.

BREAKTHROUGH & NOW

TV grind: The Americans, Blue Bloods, American Odyssey, Unbreakable Kimmy Schmidt. Film: Chuck, The Glass Castle (2017) as Young Lori Walls - where Woody Harrelson inspired her to go vegan.

In 2017 at 14, she joined Stranger Things Season 2 as Max Mayfield - the redheaded skateboarder from California. Season 4's Vecna / Running Up That Hill scene (2022) became TV history with 1B+ Spotify streams revival.

Now: Critics Choice nominee for The Whale (2022), scream-queen lead in Fear Street 1978, Taylor Swift's All Too Well muse, rock star in O'Dessa (2025), Tony nominee for John Proctor Is the Villain (2025), and cast in MCU Spider-Man: Brand New Day (July 31, 2026).

Fun facts: Natural redhead, loves skateboarding IRL, has dog Tina, besties with Millie Bobby Brown & Maya Hawke.
