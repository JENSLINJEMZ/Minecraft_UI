ABOUT THE GAME

What is Minecraft?

Minecraft is a sandbox video game created by Swedish developer Markus "Notch" Persson and developed by Mojang Studios. Players explore a world made entirely of blocks, gathering resources to build structures, craft tools, and survive the monsters that come out at night.

Since its full release in 2011, Minecraft has grown into one of the best-selling video games of all time, with a huge community that creates adventure maps, redstone machines, mods, and even entire cities. It's played on PC, consoles, and mobile devices, and it's loved by players of all ages.

2009

Classic version released as a simple prototype, quickly gaining popularity online.

2011

Full version launches on November 18, officially leaving beta and reaching PC players.

2014

Microsoft acquires Mojang and Minecraft, bringing it to Xbox and many more platforms.

2020

Spin-off Minecraft Dungeons launches, expanding the franchise into new genres.

GAME MODES

Play Your Way

Every adventure starts with a choice. Pick the mode that matches your playstyle.

Survival Mode

Gather resources, manage your health and hunger, and fight off mobs as you build your way through the world.

  • Collect wood, stone, iron, and diamonds
  • Craft tools, armor, and weapons
  • Survive the night when hostile mobs spawn
  • Build shelters, farms, and bases
⛏️

Creative Mode

Unlimited resources, instant building, and the freedom to fly. Perfect for architects and artists.

  • Access every block in the game
  • No health, hunger, or damage
  • Fly freely across the map
  • Build massive projects without limits
🏰

Adventure Mode

Designed for custom maps and story-driven experiences, where players can't freely break or place blocks.

  • Restricted block interaction
  • Ideal for puzzle and parkour maps
  • Great for storytelling and mini-games
  • Community maps bring endless variety
🗺️

Hardcore Mode

One life. No second chances. Once you die, your world is gone forever. Only for the brave.

  • Permanent death, with no respawn
  • Always locked to Hard difficulty
  • Ultimate test of skill and planning
  • Bragging rights for the survivors

DIMENSIONS

Three Worlds to Conquer

Every Minecraft world is split into three dimensions, each with its own dangers and treasures.

🌳

THE OVERWORLD

The starting point of your journey. Forests, deserts, snowy tundras, oceans, and more. Survive the day-night cycle and explore procedurally generated terrain.

🔥

THE NETHER

A fiery underworld of lava lakes, glowstone, and fortresses. Reach it through an obsidian portal and beware of ghasts, blazes, and piglins.

🐉

THE END

A mysterious void dimension home to the Ender Dragon. Find strongholds, prepare your gear, and face the final boss of the main game.
